# PRODUCT.md — International Association for the Defense of Flowers

## register: brand

## Product Purpose
The website is the primary digital presence of the IADF (International Association for the Defense of Flowers), presenting the Proveneya & Nahiara literary and transmedia ecosystem. The site tells the story of a project uniting 195 national flowers and their pollinators through an illustrated book, a 3D floral archive, a card game, and robotic museum exhibitions. It invites readers, educators, publishers, and conservation partners worldwide to explore and connect.

## Users
- Readers and families who discovered the Nahiara book
- Educators and school librarians seeking conservation-themed literature
- International publishers and editorial partners
- Museum curators and event organizers for robotic exhibitions
- Conservationists and biodiversity advocates
- Press and media contacts

## Brand Voice
- Lyrical, hopeful, warmly international
- Nature-reverent but not earnest-boring — there's wonder and magic
- Inclusive across cultures (5 languages: EN, ES, FR, DE, ZH)
- Literary and tactile — the book is the heart

## Brand Aesthetic Lane
Botanical illustration meets transmedia storytelling. The visual language references a 19th-century natural history atlas crossed with a modern cultural institution (think: Rijksmuseum digital, Jardin des Plantes, a luminous herbarium). Warm deep greens, earthy gold accents, cream tones. The 3D logo and archive introduce a contemporary technological counterpoint. Type should feel archival and specific — not tech-startup.

## Anti-References
- Generic startup/SaaS landing pages
- Dark-mode "cool" tech aesthetics
- Flat icon + heading + text card grids
- Neon botanical aesthetics
- Anything that reads as a charity donation page template

## Key Calls to Action
1. Explore the 3D Floral Archive (aquamundi.org)
2. Contact the association for partnerships / press
3. Download the press kit

## Color Notes
Green is the brand hue. The current palette is flat green-500 / gray-200. Should be richer: deep forest green, warm cream, aged parchment gold. Not lime, not neon.
